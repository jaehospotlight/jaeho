There are fundamentally 2 problems with the agency industry, specifically within industries that rely on past results. 

1. Competition is based on past capabilities, instead of existent 'alpha'
2. If agencies are differentiated through 'alpha', the founder who once discovered 'alpha' now spends more time on 'management' as the people problem scales exponentially. 

This 'truth' is recognized, but instead of change, it's accepted as the status quo. 

**Solution on 1:**
1. As a company grows, your #1 KPI needs to be % of success. Failure is a guarantee. 
	1. The reality is no agency can 100% guarantee success within a discrete time length (in an infinite timelength, with the brand having PMF & PCF (product channel fit) the results may differ).

**Solution on 2:** 
Increase the output per person. 
1. Have a culture/system that enables you to compound with more people. If each additional person is n^3 while management is n^2, your delta is n^3 - n^2, which means your growth is still n^3. 
	1. Pros: You're building a culture.  Cons: You have to fire a lot. Your hiring becomes more subjective due to 'cultural' fit. 
Reduce the people problem. 
2. Hire less people. If each individual can do more
	1. Pros: You can't have as many people problem, if there aren't that many people. Cons: Firing becomes harder in the 10 to 20. 

What does this mean for Spotlight:
1. We need to re-orient the KPI. 
2.  education becomes a higher priority. Important question: is on the job learning worse than dedicated education? 
3. We need to increase the bar at Spotlight. If spawn rate of an ideal  candidate is 1% (self driven & intelligent) and an ok candidate is 5% (intelligent). 
4. People don't need to be AI pilled, **but they need to 'utilize' AI workflows.** Eventually, those who don't get pilled will be promoted at a lower rate.
